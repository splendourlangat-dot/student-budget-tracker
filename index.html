Student Savings Tips

Start small. Even KSh 20 or KSh 50 is progress when KSh 100 is not possible.

Be consistent. Record savings on the date you actually put the money aside.

Protect your essentials. Food, transport, school needs, and other necessities come first. Do not borrow money just to meet a savings target.

Savings are tracked separately from expenses. Recording KSh 100 in savings reduces your available balance by KSh 100 and increases your savings by KSh 100.
